Emergency technology · Experimental prototype

Rescuer Sathi

Built for the moments when connectivity disappears. Rescuer Sathi saves an SOS locally before transmission and coordinates the journey from emergency alert to verified responder.

  • Three trained AI modelsTrained injury detection, injury classification, and burn-severity classification models, integrated with TensorFlow Lite for on-device inference in an experimental screening workflow.
  • Offline SOSEncrypted local queue preserves alerts through lost connectivity and app restarts, with automatic retry when Internet access returns.
  • Nearby relayAndroid Nearby Connections supports encrypted store-and-forward relay through nearby devices; field validation is pending.
  • Rescue workflowVerified responders, consent-based medical snapshots, and Guardian fall/crash heuristics connect preparation with response.
